❓ Frequently Asked Questions
Why is my internet connection slow? ▼
Slow internet connections may be caused by heavy network traffic, high network usage, or issues with the Internet Service Provider (ISP).
What should I do if I have no internet? ▼
Please contact the official Zenfast Voucher Seller in your area to determine the cause of the internet outage.
How can I report a payment? ▼
Select “Payment” in the form, attach a screenshot of your payment receipt, provide your contact information, and clearly describe your concern.
How can I follow up on my concern? ▼
Use the Ticket Tracker widget above and enter your unique ticket number to check the status of your concern. Once your concern has been resolved, we will notify you immediately through your preferred contact method.
How do I contact customer service? ▼
You can submit a ticket right here on our portal, or check our contact information card below for direct assistance hours.
